How it works

From mandate to delivery in four steps

A complete purchase, negotiated and settled in one sitting.

01

Set your mandate

Name the item, your budget and the price you want. The agent never negotiates past those limits.

02

Agents haggle in parallel

Every seller is approached at once. Your agent uses real competing offers as leverage, round after round.

03

The best offer wins

Offers are ranked on price, deposit and delivery. The deal that costs you least overall takes the win.

04

Pay the deposit, settle on delivery

Approve once in PayPal. The deposit is captured up front and the balance only after you confirm delivery.

About this demo

Honest by design

Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. Everything below is here so the demo never overpromises.

Sandbox payments

PayPal runs in sandbox mode end to end. No real money moves, and no live account is charged.

Simulated sellers

The default seller agents use sample catalogs. The Seller Policy console shows how a real merchant would join.

Recorded benchmark

Benchmark figures come from a fixed set of recorded negotiations, not a live market sample.

Session memory

Deals and compiled policies stay available for this demo session. They are not a permanent record.
