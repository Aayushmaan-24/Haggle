What your agent enforces

Clear rules, every round

Your policy is checked before every offer. Recorded replays follow their original script, so they do not use saved policies.

Your floor holds

Your agent will never accept below the floor in your policy. If the buyer cannot reach it, the seller walks away.

Deposit is fixed

The deposit percentage you set is what the buyer pays up front through PayPal. It does not change mid-negotiation.

Delivery is fixed

Your delivery time is locked for every round. This buyer looks for 7 days or fewer.

The buyer has limits too

The buyer never goes above its budget, and keeps pushing toward its target while rounds remain.

Policies are kept for this browser session. Building a new policy does not replace the old one.
